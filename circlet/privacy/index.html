Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-07

Circlet does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • Measurements and saved sizes stay on your device. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Preferences you attach to a share card stay on your device until you choose to share them, using your phone's own share sheet, to a person you pick.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Circlet receives only whether the purchase is active. We never see your name, email, or payment details.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, no accounts.
  • Circlet makes no network requests of its own.

If a future version changes any of this, this page will say so before it ships.
