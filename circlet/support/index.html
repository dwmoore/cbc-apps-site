Circlet: Ring Sizer measures ring size with a ring you already own, or with a strip of paper if you don't.

Getting an exact reading

  • Set your ring flat on the screen and match its inside edge to the drawn ring's inside edge. Drag the bead, pinch, or scrub the ruler.
  • If Circlet says your screen isn't recognised, open Settings → Calibrate with a card and match the outline to a bank card's short edge. One time.
  • Measure in the afternoon at room temperature; fingers change through the day.
  • Without a ring: wrap a strip of paper snugly where the ring will sit, mark the overlap, and lay it on the ruler with its end at 0. If the knuckle is larger than the base, size between the two, nearer the knuckle.
